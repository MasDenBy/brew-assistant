# Fuggle Hops

**Type:** Ingredient (Hop)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Overview
Fuggle is a classic English hop, discovered in Kent in the 1860s. It has a mild, earthy, woody, slightly fruity character and was the dominant British bittering/flavor hop for over a century. It defines the profile of many British bitters, porters, and stouts.

## Key Specs
- Alpha acid: 3.5–5.5%
- Beta acid: 1.5–3.0%
- Common uses: bittering and flavor in British styles; restrained late additions
- Flavor/aroma: earthy, woody, mild fruity, grassy
- Origin: United Kingdom

## Substitutions
- **East Kent Goldings (EKG)** — the closest English substitute; floral and slightly sweeter
- **Willamette** (USA) — similar mild earthy character, slightly more spice
- **Challenger** — English hop, a bit more assertive/spicy
- **Saaz** — different origin but shares the mild, earthy-spicy direction for lagers

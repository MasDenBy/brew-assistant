# Pale 2-Row Malt (American)

**Type:** Ingredient (Malt)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Overview
American pale 2-row is the workhorse base malt of craft brewing — a clean, slightly malty foundation used at 70–100% of the grist for ales and lagers alike. It has enough diastatic power to convert itself and most adjuncts, with a pale straw color and a light bready/cereal character.

## Key Specs
- Color: 1.8–2.5 °L
- Diastatic power: high (fully self-converting)
- Typical usage: 70–100% of the grist
- Flavor: clean, slightly malty, bready, neutral

## Common Substitutions
- **Maris Otter** (English) — richer, more biscuity; use when more malt character is wanted
- **Munich malt (light)** — more malty depth; replace up to ~25% to add complexity
- **Pilsner malt** — lighter, more delicate; slightly more fermentation activity; good substitute in hoppy styles
- **Golden Promise** (Scottish) — richer, honey-like; a direct 1:1 substitute with more flavor

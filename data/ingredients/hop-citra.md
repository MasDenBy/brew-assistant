# Citra Hops

**Type:** Ingredient (Hop)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Overview
Citra (HBC 566) is one of the most popular modern American hops, released in 2008 by Hop Breeding Company. It delivers intense tropical fruit — mango, passion fruit, lychee — plus grapefruit and lime. It is a cornerstone hop for NEIPAs and hazy pale ales.

## Key Specs
- Alpha acid: 11.0–13.0%
- Beta acid: 3.5–4.5%
- Common uses: late boil, whirlpool, dry hop — aroma first, but its high alpha makes it a capable bittering hop too
- Flavor/aroma: mango, passion fruit, citrus, tropical
- Origin: USA

## Substitutions
- **Mosaic** — closest overall: tropical, berry, citrus blend with similar intensity
- **Galaxy** (Australian) — passion fruit and citrus; excellent substitute when available
- **Simcoe** — more pine/resin but same tropical-citrus punch
- **Amarillo** — citrus-forward stand-in, less tropical

# US-05 Yeast (SafAle US-05)

**Type:** Ingredient (Yeast)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Overview
SafAle US-05 is a dry American ale yeast derived from the AltBier strain, famous for a clean, neutral fermentation that lets hops and malt speak. It is the default choice for American ales, IPAs, and pale ales — functionally equivalent to WLP001 and Wyeast 1056.

## Key Specs
- Attenuation: 78–82% (medium-high)
- Flocculation: medium (slightly low; may need time or finings to clarify)
- Alcohol tolerance: 10–11%
- Recommended temperature: 15–22 °C (59–72 °F)
- Flavor contribution: very clean, neutral, minimal esters/phenols

## Common Substitutions
- **WLP001 (California Ale)** — liquid equivalent, same clean character
- **Wyeast 1056 (American Ale)** — liquid equivalent
- **Nottingham (dry)** — similar clean profile, slightly higher attenuation and flocculation
- **S-04 (dry)** — English ale yeast; flocculates harder, adds slight fruitiness — a workable but not identical swap

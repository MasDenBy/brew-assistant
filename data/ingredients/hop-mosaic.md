# Mosaic Hops

**Type:** Ingredient (Hop)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Overview
Mosaic (HBC 369) is a modern American hop released in 2012 by Hop Breeding Company (a daughter of Simcoe). It is famous for a complex blend of tropical fruit, berry, citrus, and herbal notes — often described as "Citra with berries and herbs."

## Key Specs
- Alpha acid: 11.5–13.5%
- Beta acid: 3.0–4.5%
- Common uses: dry hop, whirlpool, late boil; also effective for bittering
- Flavor/aroma: mango, tropical, berry, citrus, herbal
- Origin: USA

## Substitutions
- **Citra** — closest single-hop replacement; more pure tropical-citrus
- **Simcoe** — parent hop; more pine/resin but similar complexity
- **Galaxy** — tropical-passion fruit focus
- **Amarillo + Citra blend** — approximates Mosaic's layered fruit character

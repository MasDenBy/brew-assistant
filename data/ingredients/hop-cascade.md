# Cascade Hops

**Type:** Ingredient (Hop)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Overview
Cascade is the iconic American aroma hop, bred by USDA/OSU and released in 1972. It defined the American craft beer revolution with its floral, citrus (grapefruit) character and moderate bitterness. It is the signature hop of Sierra Nevada Pale Ale.

## Key Specs
- Alpha acid: 4.5–7.0%
- Beta acid: 3.5–5.5%
- Common uses: flavor and aroma additions, dry hopping; also used for moderate bittering
- Flavor/aroma: floral, grapefruit, citrus, slight spice
- Origin: USA

## Substitutions
- **Centennial** — the closest all-around substitute ("Cascade with more alpha"); floral-citrus, slightly more bitter
- **Amarillo** — similar citrus/floral profile, more orange/tangerine
- **Citra** — more tropical but works where citrus character is the goal
- **Willamette** — milder, earthier; a good domestic replacement when a softer hop is wanted

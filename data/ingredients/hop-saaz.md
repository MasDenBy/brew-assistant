# Saaz Hops

**Type:** Ingredient (Hop)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Overview
Saaz is the quintessential noble hop from the Czech Republic (Žatec/Ústí region). It has a mild, earthy, spicy, herbal character with low alpha acids, and is the defining hop of Bohemian Pilsners and many Czech lagers. A "true" noble hop with a history spanning centuries.

## Key Specs
- Alpha acid: 2.0–4.0%
- Beta acid: 4.0–6.0%
- Common uses: bittering and flavor in lagers; any role where a gentle noble hop is wanted
- Flavor/aroma: spicy, herbal, earthy, mild floral
- Origin: Czech Republic

## Substitutions
- **Hallertau Mittelfrüh** — the classic German noble substitute; very similar mild-spicy profile
- **Tettnang** — German noble, slightly more floral
- **Spalt** — German noble, earthy and mild
- **Fuggle** — English rather than noble, but earthy-mild in a similar direction (slightly more woody)

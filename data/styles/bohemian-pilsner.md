# Bohemian Pilsner (Czech Pilsner)

**Type:** Style (BJCP 5A / 5B)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Description
Bohemian Pilsner is a characterful, amber-gold German-style pale lager with a soft, complex maltiness and a pronounced, spicy noble hop bitterness. It is maltier and softer than a German Pilsner, with Saaz hops defining its floral-spicy aroma.

## Typical Ranges
- OG: 1.044–1.056
- FG: 1.013–1.017
- ABV: 4.2–5.4%
- IBU: 30–45
- SRM: 3.5–6

## Characteristic Ingredients
- Czech Pilsner malt (or a pale malt with a short decoction boil for depth)
- Saaz hops are the signature: mild, noble, floral-spicy bittering and flavor
- Czech lager yeast (WLP800 / Wyeast 2001 / fermentis W-34/70 works as a clean substitute)
- Soft water with low sulfate

## Brewing Notes
Decoction mashing (or a step mash) builds the bready malt complexity. A extended cold lagering (4–8 weeks) clarifies and smooths the beer. Saaz substitutions: other noble hops — Hallertau Mittelfrüh, Tettnang, Spalt — are the closest replacements.

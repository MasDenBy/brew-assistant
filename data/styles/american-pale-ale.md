# American Pale Ale

**Type:** Style (BJCP 18A)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Description
American Pale Ale is a light-bodied, hop-forward but balanced American ale. It is less aggressive than IPA, with firm hop bitterness, a floral/citrus American hop character, and just enough malt sweetness to support it. Sierra Nevada Pale Ale is the classic reference point.

## Typical Ranges
- OG: 1.045–1.060
- FG: 1.010–1.015
- ABV: 4.5–6.2%
- IBU: 30–50
- SRM: 5–10

## Characteristic Ingredients
- American 2-row pale malt; light crystal malt (20–40L) for color and body
- American hops: Cascade, Centennial, Chinook, Simcoe, Amarillo
- Clean American ale yeast (WLP001 / US-05 / Wyeast 1056)
- Moderate sulfate water for a crisp bitter finish

## Brewing Notes
A straightforward single-hop or two-hop beer. Cascade alone gives the classic floral-citrus profile. A restrained bittering charge plus late/flameout and dry hop additions gives aroma without harshness.

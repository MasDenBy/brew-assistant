# American Amber Ale

**Type:** Style (BJCP 19A)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Description
American Amber Ale is a malt-accented but hoppy American ale with a caramel-malt sweetness balanced by a firm American hop presence. It is darker than a pale ale, with toasty, caramel notes and a medium body — a bridge between pale ale and brown ale.

## Typical Ranges
- OG: 1.045–1.060
- FG: 1.010–1.015
- ABV: 4.5–6.2%
- IBU: 25–40
- SRM: 10–17 (amber to light copper)

## Characteristic Ingredients
- American 2-row base with 10–20% crystal malt (40–60L) for caramel color and sweetness; some Victory/Biscuit malt for toastiness
- American hops: Cascade, Centennial, Willamette, Columbus — firm bittering and a visible hop presence
- Clean American ale yeast (WLP001 / US-05)
- Moderate sulfate water

## Brewing Notes
Balance leans slightly to malt but hops must be present — too sweet without hops drifts toward British amber ale. Keep crystal malt in check to avoid cloying sweetness; a mash at 66–67 °C helps dryness.

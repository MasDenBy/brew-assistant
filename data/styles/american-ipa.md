# American IPA

**Type:** Style (BJCP 21A)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Description
American IPA is a decidedly hoppy and bitter, moderately strong American ale. It showcases American hop varieties with citrus, floral, pine, or resinous qualities. The style is a balance between malt backbone and aggressive hopping, but hops clearly dominate.

## Typical Ranges
- OG: 1.056–1.075
- FG: 1.008–1.014
- ABV: 5.5–7.5%
- IBU: 40–70
- SRM: 6–14

## Characteristic Ingredients
- Pale malt base (American 2-row), often with a small amount of crystal malt (20–60L) for color and light caramel sweetness
- American hops are central: Citra, Mosaic, Centennial, Cascade, Simcoe, Amarillo
- Clean American ale yeast that lets hops shine (e.g., WLP001 / US-05)
- Water chemistry moderate sulfate to accentuate hop bitterness

## Brewing Notes
Dry hopping amplifies aroma without adding bitterness. Bittering additions are typically Magnum or a clean high-alpha hop; flavor and aroma additions of signature American varieties define the character.

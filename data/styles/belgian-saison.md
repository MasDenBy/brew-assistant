# Belgian Saison

**Type:** Style (BJCP 25B)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Description
Saison is a highly carbonated, dry, refreshing Belgian ale with fruity esters, spicy phenols, and a firm hop bitterness. Historically a farmhouse ale brewed in winter for summer drinking, it is dry, effervescent, and complex — peppery yeast character is its signature.

## Typical Ranges
- OG: 1.048–1.065
- FG: 1.002–1.012 (very dry)
- ABV: 3.5–7.0% (most 6–7%)
- IBU: 25–45
- SRM: 5–14 (gold to light amber)

## Characteristic Ingredients
- Pilsner malt base; often includes 5–15% wheat or spelt for haze and body
- Sugar additions (10–20%) thin the body for dryness
- European/American hops: Styrian Goldings, East Kent Goldings, Saaz, Willamette, Cascade
- Belgian saison yeast (WLP565 / Wyeast 3724 / fermentis BE-134 or T-58): spicy, peppery, fruity; ferment warm (22–30 °C)

## Brewing Notes
Yeast choice and fermentation temperature define the style. BE-134 attenuates fully for a bone-dry finish. Dry hopping is optional and light. Carbonation is high (3.0–3.5+ volumes CO₂) — bottle condition with plenty of priming sugar and sturdy bottles.

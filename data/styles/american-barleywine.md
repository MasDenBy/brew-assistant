# American Barleywine

**Type:** Style (BJCP 22C)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Description
American Barleywine is a massive, strong ale with intense malt complexity — caramel, toffee, dark fruit — and a bold American hop character. Very high gravity, full body, and high alcohol warmth. It ages for years, evolving like wine (hence the name).

## Typical Ranges
- OG: 1.080–1.120
- FG: 1.016–1.030
- ABV: 8.0–12.0%+
- IBU: 50–100
- SRM: 10–19 (amber to copper)

## Characteristic Ingredients
- Large grist of American 2-row with significant crystal malt (60–120L) and specialty malts (Victory, Chocolate for color)
- Liberal sugar additions are NOT typical; malt complexity is the point
- Heavy hopping: Chinook, Columbus, Centennial, Simcoe — high-alpha bittering plus late additions
- Clean American ale yeast with high alcohol tolerance; pitch big and oxygenate well

## Brewing Notes
A long, slow fermentation is normal — consider a big starter or two-stage fermentation. Bottle or keg condition for at least 6–12 months; flavors meld and alcohol harshness fades with age. High OG means careful yeast health and oxygenation are critical.

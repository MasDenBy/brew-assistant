# New England IPA (Hazy IPA)

**Type:** Style (BJCP 21B Specialty IPA: New England IPA)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Description
New England IPA is a hazy, juicy American IPA with low bitterness, intense tropical and citrus hop aroma, and a full, soft mouthfeel. The haze comes from high protein grains, hop creep, and yeast/oxidation factors — it is a defining visual trait, not a flaw.

## Typical Ranges
- OG: 1.060–1.085
- FG: 1.010–1.020
- ABV: 6.0–9.0%
- IBU: 25–60 (perceived bitterness is lower)
- SRM: 5–8 (pale straw to gold)

## Characteristic Ingredients
- High proportion of oats and/or wheat (flaked or malted) for haze and mouthfeel
- Pale malt base; minimal crystal malt
- Signature hops: Citra, Mosaic, Galaxy, Nelson Sauvin, Simcoe
- Clean American ale yeast, often with a slight fruit ester profile; some brewers use Vermont-style strains
- Water with high chloride-to-sulfate ratio to soften bitterness

## Brewing Notes
Heavy dry hopping (often multiple additions) is essential. Low bittering charge; bitterness is smooth. Oxygen sensitivity is high — cold-side oxygen exclusion matters for shelf stability.

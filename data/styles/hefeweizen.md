# German Hefeweizen

**Type:** Style (BJCP 10A)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Description
German Hefeweizen is a bright, cloudy wheat beer famous for its yeast-driven banana (isoamyl acetate) and clove (4-vinyl guaiacol) esters. Wheat malt dominates the grist, giving a soft, creamy mouthfeel and a persistent white head. Hop flavor is very low; bitterness is restrained.

## Typical Ranges
- OG: 1.044–1.053
- FG: 1.010–1.014
- ABV: 4.3–5.6%
- IBU: 8–15
- SRM: 2–6 (pale)

## Characteristic Ingredients
- 50–70% wheat malt, remainder German Pilsner or pale malt
- German noble hops (Hallertau, Tettnang, Spalt) used only for mild bittering
- Classic German wheat beer yeast: WLP300 / Wyeast 3068 / fermentis WB-06 — produces banana and clove
- Soft water, low in minerals

## Brewing Notes
Yeast management controls the ester profile: cooler fermentation (~17–19 °C) favors clove; warmer (~19–22 °C) favors banana. Ferment under modest pressure for carbonation; bottle condition with remaining yeast for the classic cloudy pour. No dry hopping — the yeast defines this beer.

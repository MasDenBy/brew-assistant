# Porter

**Type:** Style (BJCP 20A / 9C)
**Source:** Generated for Phase 1 prototype

## Description
Porter is a dark, malty American or British ale with chocolate, caramel, and light roast character, balanced by enough hop bitterness to avoid sweetness. Darker and stronger than a dry stout but smoother and less roast-forward — it sits between amber ale and stout.

## Typical Ranges
- OG: 1.048–1.065
- FG: 1.012–1.018
- ABV: 4.8–6.5%
- IBU: 25–50
- SRM: 22–35

## Characteristic Ingredients
- Pale malt base with chocolate malt, brown malt, and a touch of caramel/crystal malt (60–80L)
- Some brewers add a small amount of roasted barley for dryness
- Hops: Fuggle, East Kent Goldings, Challenger (British); American versions use Cascade or Willamette
- British or American ale yeast — WLP002/Wyeast 1968 for a malty British profile, WLP001 for a cleaner American take

## Brewing Notes
Mash slightly warmer (67–68 °C) for residual body. Brown malt gives the classic " porter " complexity; overdoing roast turns it into stout territory. Very good base for barrel aging and adjunct variants (chocolate, vanilla, coffee).
